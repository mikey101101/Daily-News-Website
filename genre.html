Sandbox

Sandbox games are a genre of video games that emphasize open-ended gameplay and player freedom. In sandbox games, players are given a large, open world to explore and interact with, often with few restrictions or limitations on what they can do. The term "sandbox" comes from the idea that players can create their own experiences within the game, much like a child playing in a sandbox.

A sandbox game is a type of video game that gives players a high degree of freedom to explore, interact with the environment and choose their own objectives. Rather than following a strictly linear path, players are often free to experiment and approach the game in different ways.

History

Sandbox games developed from early open-ended games that allowed players greater freedom than traditional level-based games. As game worlds became larger and more complex, developers began creating games built around exploration, player choice and interaction with open environments.
