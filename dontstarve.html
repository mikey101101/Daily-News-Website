GAME ARCHIVE // SURVIVAL // 2013

Don't Starve

Don't Starve is a survival game developed by Klei Entertainment and released in 2013. Players are placed into a strange and hostile wilderness where survival depends on exploration, resource gathering, crafting and preparation.

Game Data //

Released: 2013

Developer: Klei Entertainment

Publisher: Klei Entertainment

Original Platforms: PC, Mac and Linux

Genre: Survival

Archive // 002

Don't Starve represents survival games where staying alive is itself the player's main objective.

There is no traditional sequence of levels. Instead, players must learn how the world works and become increasingly prepared for its dangers.

Overview //

The player begins with very little equipment and must immediately explore the environment for useful materials.

Grass, twigs, food and other resources can be collected and transformed into tools, fires, weapons and structures.

Survival becomes progressively more difficult as the player encounters darkness, hostile creatures and changing seasons.

Survival Systems //

Hunger, health and sanity must all be managed. Allowing any of these systems to deteriorate can place the player in increasing danger.

Daylight is also a resource. Players must use the daytime to explore and gather supplies before darkness arrives.

Crafting allows basic materials to gradually become more advanced equipment, making preparation one of the most important parts of the game.

Procedural Worlds //

The game's world is procedurally generated. Resources, environments and important locations can therefore appear differently between playthroughs.

This prevents players from relying entirely on memorised routes. They must explore each world and adapt their strategy to the resources they discover.

Why It Matters //

Don't Starve demonstrates the development of survival into a genre where interconnected systems generate the challenge.

Hunger, darkness, weather, crafting and exploration constantly interact, requiring the player to prepare for problems before they become dangerous.
