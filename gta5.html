// OVERVIEW

Grand Theft Auto V is an open-world action-adventure game developed by Rockstar North and released in 2013. Set primarily in the fictional city of Los Santos and the surrounding region of Blaine County, the game provides players with a large environment that can be explored freely using vehicles, aircraft or on foot.

Unlike games that restrict players to a fixed sequence of levels, Grand Theft Auto V combines structured story missions with open-ended exploration. Players can move throughout the world, participate in optional activities, interact with different locations and characters, purchase property and vehicles, or simply explore independently of the main story.

The game also uses three playable protagonists: Michael De Santa, Franklin Clinton and Trevor Philips. Players can switch between these characters during different parts of the game, allowing the story and open world to be experienced from several perspectives.

// HISTORY

Grand Theft Auto V was developed by Rockstar North following the release of Grand Theft Auto IV in 2008. Rockstar had already established the Grand Theft Auto series as one of the most recognisable examples of open-world game design, but GTA V significantly expanded the scale and variety of its playable environment.

The game was officially released in September 2013. Its setting returned the series to the fictional state of San Andreas, with Los Santos drawing inspiration from Los Angeles and Southern California. The environment combined a dense city with countryside, mountains, highways, beaches and smaller settlements.

GTA V continued to receive attention long after its original release. Later versions brought the game to newer generations of hardware, while its large open environment and online component contributed to its unusually long lifespan.

ARCHIVE NOTE // 002

// OPEN WORLD DESIGN

Exploration is a major part of Grand Theft Auto V's design. Los Santos and Blaine County form a connected open world containing urban areas, countryside, mountains, highways and coastal environments.

Rather than selecting isolated levels from a menu, players physically travel between locations within the game world. Cars, motorcycles, boats, aircraft and other forms of transport allow players to navigate the environment in different ways.

This structure allows exploration to become part of normal gameplay. Travelling to a mission can lead the player towards optional activities, random events or unfamiliar locations.

The world therefore functions as more than a background for missions. It becomes an important part of the player's experience and gives players reasons to interact with the environment outside the main storyline.

SYSTEM ANALYSIS // 003

// PLAYER FREEDOM

GTA V gives players considerable freedom over how they spend their time within the game world. Story missions provide a structured narrative, but completing those missions is only one part of the overall experience. Players are regularly able to leave the main story and explore Los Santos independently.

Optional activities include purchasing vehicles and property, participating in races, exploring remote areas, completing side missions and interacting with different parts of the environment. This creates a contrast between directed gameplay and player-driven activity.

The three-protagonist system also changes how the player interacts with the world. Michael, Franklin and Trevor occupy different locations, have different backgrounds and participate in different activities. Switching between them gives the player another form of control over how the game's world and narrative are experienced.

HISTORICAL RECORD // 004

// HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

Grand Theft Auto V represents an important stage in the development of large-scale open-world games. Its world combines a large geographical area with missions, characters, traffic, activities and environmental detail, demonstrating how increasingly complex systems could exist inside one connected playable space.

The game also demonstrates how open-world design can combine authored storytelling with player freedom. Rockstar provides a structured narrative, but players are not required to interact with the world in only one way. Exploration and optional activities allow individual players to create experiences outside the main story.

Its long commercial lifespan also illustrates the growing importance of persistent game worlds. Rather than disappearing shortly after its original release, GTA V continued across multiple hardware generations and remained visible within gaming culture for many years.
