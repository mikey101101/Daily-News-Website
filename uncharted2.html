GAME ARCHIVE // ADVENTURE // 2009

Uncharted 2: Among Thieves

Uncharted 2: Among Thieves is an action-adventure game developed by Naughty Dog and released for the PlayStation 3 in 2009. The game follows treasure hunter Nathan Drake on a journey involving exploration, climbing, combat and puzzle solving while searching for the legendary kingdom of Shambhala.

Game Data //

Released: 2009

Developer: Naughty Dog

Publisher: Sony Computer Entertainment

Original Platform: PlayStation 3

Genre: Action-Adventure

Archive // 002

Uncharted 2 represents the increasingly cinematic direction of adventure games during the late 2000s.

Instead of separating story and gameplay completely, the game frequently allows major cinematic events to happen while the player remains in control.

Overview //

Players control Nathan Drake through environments including cities, ruins, mountains and temples. Progression combines climbing, environmental traversal, gun combat, puzzles and scripted story sequences.

The game follows a more directed path than an open-world adventure, but its environments are designed to create a strong sense of movement and discovery.

History //

Uncharted 2 was released during a period when improvements in console hardware allowed developers to create increasingly detailed characters, animation and environments.

Naughty Dog used these developments to create an adventure influenced heavily by cinematic storytelling.

Large action sequences were integrated into playable sections, helping reduce the separation between traditional cutscenes and gameplay.

Exploration & Gameplay //

Traversal is a major part of the experience. Nathan Drake can climb walls, jump between ledges and navigate large environmental obstacles.

Exploration is combined with third-person combat and puzzle solving. These different mechanics are regularly alternated to control the pace of the adventure.

Environmental storytelling also helps communicate where the player is travelling and gives locations a stronger sense of history and scale.

Why It Matters //

Uncharted 2 demonstrated how adventure games could combine interactive gameplay with the pacing and presentation commonly associated with action films.

Its approach to character animation, scripted sequences, traversal and cinematic presentation influenced the direction of many later story-driven action-adventure games.

Historical Significance //

01

Cinematic Gameplay

Major action sequences occur while the player is still controlling the character, creating a closer connection between gameplay and cinematic presentation.

02

Character Performance

Detailed animation, dialogue and character performances helped make storytelling an important part of the adventure experience.

03

Modern Adventure

Demonstrated how traversal, combat, puzzles and narrative could be combined into a highly polished modern action-adventure game.
