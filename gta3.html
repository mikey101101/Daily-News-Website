GAME ARCHIVE // OPEN WORLD // 2001

Grand Theft Auto III

Grand Theft Auto III is an open-world action game developed by DMA Design and released in 2001. Set in Liberty City, the game places the player inside a large three-dimensional urban environment that can be freely explored between missions.

Game Data //

Released: 2001

Developer: DMA Design

Publisher: Rockstar Games

Original Platform: PlayStation 2

Genre: Open World / Action

Archive // 001

Grand Theft Auto III became an important milestone in the development and popularisation of three-dimensional open-world games.

Instead of treating the city as a background, Liberty City became a space that players could travel through and interact with.

Overview //

Players explore Liberty City on foot or by using a variety of vehicles. Story missions provide structured objectives, but the player is generally free to travel around the available city between missions.

This creates two layers of play: structured missions designed by the developers and unscripted activities created by the player's interaction with the city.

Open-World Design //

Liberty City contains roads, pedestrians, traffic, buildings and different districts that create the impression of a functioning urban environment.

Vehicles allow players to travel through the world quickly, while the road network gives the city a recognisable physical structure.

Players are not permanently restricted to the route required by the next mission, making exploration and experimentation important parts of the experience.

Player Freedom //

The player can temporarily ignore the main story and explore Liberty City instead.

This freedom allows players to interact with vehicles, police, pedestrians and the environment in ways that are not necessarily connected to a mission.

The resulting unscripted situations became an important part of the appeal of open-world games.

Why It Matters //

Grand Theft Auto III helped demonstrate the possibilities of large three-dimensional urban environments.

Its combination of exploration, driving, missions and player freedom became highly influential on later open-world games.

Historical Significance //

01

3D City

Liberty City demonstrated how an urban environment could become a large explorable 3D game space.

02

Player Freedom

Players could leave the main mission structure and explore or experiment within the city.

03

Emergent Gameplay

Interacting systems could create unpredictable situations outside the game's scripted missions.
