// GAME COMPARISON 01

Minecraft vs Terraria

Two influential sandbox games released in 2011, sharing creative freedom, exploration and crafting, but offering different approaches to gameplay.

Minecraft

Release: 2011

Developer: Mojang

Genre: Sandbox / Survival

Core Mechanics

Building, crafting, exploration and survival.

Historical Influence

Helped popularise creative sandbox worlds and player-built experiences.

Terraria

Release: 2011

Developer: Re-Logic

Genre: Sandbox / Action-Adventure

Core Mechanics

Building, crafting, exploration and combat.

Historical Influence

Combined sandbox freedom with structured progression, exploration and boss encounters.

// COMPARISON SUMMARY

Both games encourage creativity and exploration. Minecraft focuses on 3D building and open-ended experiences, while Terraria uses a 2D perspective with greater emphasis on combat, equipment progression and boss encounters.

// GAME COMPARISON 02

GTA V vs Red Dead Redemption 2

Two open-world games developed by Rockstar Games, offering immersive environments, storytelling and exploration across different historical settings.

Grand Theft Auto V

Release: 2013

Developer: Rockstar North

Genre: Open World / Action-Adventure

Core Mechanics

Driving, shooting, missions, character switching and open-world exploration.

Historical Influence

Demonstrated the commercial potential of expansive open worlds, cinematic storytelling and long-running online multiplayer experiences.

Red Dead Redemption 2

Release: 2018

Developer: Rockstar Studios

Genre: Open World / Action-Adventure

Core Mechanics

Horse riding, shooting, hunting, exploration, survival elements and character interactions.

Historical Influence

Advanced expectations for environmental detail, immersive world design and cinematic storytelling in open-world games.

// COMPARISON SUMMARY

Both games offer expansive open worlds and narrative-driven gameplay. GTA V focuses on modern urban environments, fast-paced action and multiple protagonists, while Red Dead Redemption 2 emphasises a slower, more immersive experience set in the American frontier during 1899.

// GAME COMPARISON 03

Super Mario Bros. vs Sonic the Hedgehog

Two iconic platform games that helped define the genre through distinctive movement, level design and character identities.

Super Mario Bros.

Release: 1985

Developer: Nintendo R&D4

Genre: Platformer / Side-Scrolling

Core Mechanics

Running, jumping, collecting power-ups, defeating enemies and navigating levels.

Historical Influence

Helped establish conventions for side-scrolling platform games, including precise movement, progressive difficulty and memorable level design.

Sonic the Hedgehog

Release: 1991

Developer: Sonic Team

Genre: Platformer / Side-Scrolling

Core Mechanics

High-speed running, jumping, collecting rings, defeating enemies and momentum-based movement.

Historical Influence

Popularised speed-focused platforming and became a defining franchise for Sega, contributing to the console competition of the early 1990s.

// COMPARISON SUMMARY

Both games helped shape the platforming genre. Super Mario Bros. emphasises controlled movement, precision and carefully structured levels, while Sonic the Hedgehog focuses on speed, momentum and multiple routes through stages. Their different approaches demonstrate how platforming evolved across console generations.
